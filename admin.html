The new look

A cleaner, Apple-style look for the hub. Everyone has it now, and phones always use it. If you would rather have the classic look on your computer, untick the box — it only changes it for you, and you can switch back any time.

What the hub tells you about. In the hub = the bell (and an email to the address you sign in with). On my phone = a notification that pops up on your phone or computer, even with the hub closed. These switches only change it for you; everyone sets their own.
