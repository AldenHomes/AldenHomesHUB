Proposed suite

The full job-costing & project-management vision

Five connected apps that replace the spreadsheet-and-Buildertrend workflow — selections drive the budget, historic builds sharpen it, invoices track actuals, and one production hub runs schedules, contractors, and jobsite photos. Every app below is clickable with sample data.

This is a separate concept demo — it does not touch the live Alden Hub apps you run every day. All houses, numbers, and contractors here are made up so you can walk the whole flow before anything gets built for real.
